FolioOS - writing fonts
=======================

Free fonts (SIL Open Font License; Apache 2.0 for Special Elite) chosen for writing
and reading for hours. They come with FolioOS: each family is a folder here, with its
license. They appear in Settings > Appearance > Text font and in the theme editor
(text font and interface font).

INCLUDED FAMILIES
  Serif, for long prose
    Literata ............ designed for e-books; excellent on screens
    Lora ................ calligraphic, warm
    EB Garamond ......... classic, printed-book feel
    PT Serif ............ sober, with true italic and bold
    Gentium Book Plus ... very legible, great with accents
    IBM Plex Serif ...... modern, firm stroke
  iA Writer style (all three with bold and italic)
    iA Writer Quattro ... proportional, comfortable to read
    iA Writer Duo ....... semi-monospaced
    iA Writer Mono ...... monospaced
  Typewriter
    Special Elite ....... worn typewriter
    Cutive Mono ......... clean typewriter

  Literata, Lora and EB Garamond are variable fonts: FolioOS uses the regular
  weight and synthesizes bold.

  A family you never use can be deleted (remove its folder); it comes back the next
  time you copy FolioOS.zip to the drive.

YOUR OWN FONTS
  Any .ttf works (.otf files with CFF outlines are not supported). Download one,
  for example from fonts.google.com, and put the .ttf in FOLIO\FONTS (or a
  subfolder). Regular, Bold, Italic and BoldItalic files of the same family
  become a single family. Restart FolioOS to see it.
