FolioOS — how to install it on a USB drive (no disk image needed)
=================================================================

You do not need to write a disk image or erase the whole drive. FolioOS takes about
7 MB; the rest of the drive stays free for whatever you like.

1) The drive must be FAT32 (that is what UEFI firmware can read).
   - Drives up to 32 GB almost always come as FAT32: skip to step 2.
   - To check on Windows: right-click the drive > Properties > "File system".
   - If it is exFAT or NTFS (common above 32 GB), format it as FAT32
     (this erases what is on it):
       Windows, up to 32 GB: right-click > Format > FAT32.
       Windows, above 32 GB: use Rufus (rufus.ie), "Boot selection: Non bootable",
         "File system: FAT32".
       macOS: Disk Utility > Erase > "MS-DOS (FAT)", scheme "GUID Partition Map"
         or "Master Boot Record".
       Linux: sudo mkfs.vfat -F 32 -n FOLIO /dev/sdX1

2) Unzip this ZIP at the ROOT of the drive (the EFI, FOLIO, DOCUMENTS and MUSIC
   folders must sit directly in E:\, not inside another folder).
   Files already on the drive stay where they are.

3) Done. Start the PC with the drive plugged in, open the boot menu (F12 Dell/Lenovo,
   F9 HP, F8 Asus, Esc/F11 others) and pick the "UEFI" entry of the drive.
   If it does not show up, disable "Secure Boot" in the firmware setup (F2/Del).

EVERYTHING IS INCLUDED: FolioOS.zip already brings 11 free font families for
writing and reading and the fonts for Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Every one of
the 15 languages works out of the box; nothing else to download.

UPDATING: unzip the new version on top and confirm "replace". Only the system
(EFI\, FOLIO\FONTS\, FOLIO\LANGUAGES\, FOLIO\KEYBOARDS\), the README files and the
example DOCUMENTS\Welcome.md are replaced; your texts, music and preferences
(FOLIO\CONFIG.INI) stay.

More details in README.txt.
